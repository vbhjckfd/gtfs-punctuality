How it is measured. A departure counts when a vehicle running a trip is seen within 50 m of that trip's first stop, then drives off and is seen more than 1 km away within 10 minutes. The crossing instant is interpolated between GPS fixes (about 10 s apart). Buses that only shuffle along the kerb, or that vanish from the feed, are not counted. On time = −1…+5 min.
